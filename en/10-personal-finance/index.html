Branch 10 · Personal Finance

Personal Finance

The first nine branches looked at institutions and markets; this one turns the lens back on you. Income, saving, investing, debt, and protection are all, at heart, about managing the balance sheet of “you.” Three topics and a few disciplines: first seal off the downside risks in your finances, then let compounding go to work for you.

After this branch, you can…

Run the numbers on a long-term investment in your head with compound interest and the rule of 72; build yourself a stock-and-bond allocation keyed to your life cycle; plan debt repayment with the avalanche or snowball method and set up an emergency fund.
