Corporate Finance

Where does a company’s money come from, where does it go, and how is it split once earned? Corporate finance studies the firm’s three great financial decisions: how to spend it = the investment decision; how to raise it = the financing decision; how to distribute it = the dividend decision. Three threads, unlocked in three lessons.

After this branch, you can…

Use NPV to judge whether an investment — even a life decision — is worth it; see how leverage magnifies both returns and the risk of bankruptcy; use the price-to-earnings ratio for a quick read on whether a company is expensive.
