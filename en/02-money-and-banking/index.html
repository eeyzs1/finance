Money & Banking

Money is the lifeblood of the financial system. This branch answers three questions: what money is, how banks “create” it, and how the central bank manages it.

After this branch, you can…

① Explain to someone how the money in WeChat Pay differs from cash; ② describe how a single deposit becomes many multiples of money; ③ trace how a central bank rate cut travels, layer by layer, down to your mortgage payment.
