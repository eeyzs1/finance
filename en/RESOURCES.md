# Finance Learning Resources

> Rule: only high-credibility sources make the list; each entry notes what it covers and when to use it. Course knowledge points should be drawn from these sources first, rather than written from memory.

## Knowledge (source material)

- [Investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/)
  The world's largest finance encyclopedia — entry quality varies, but coverage is enormous. Use it for: the English definition and an intuitive explanation of any finance term; its [Term of the Day](https://www.investopedia.com/financial-term-dictionary-4769738) dictionary is the first-stop tool.
- [Wikipedia (Chinese) · Finance category](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:%E9%87%91%E8%9E%8D%E5%AD%A6) / [Finance on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance)
  The Chinese and English entries complement each other. Use it for: quickly building a conceptual frame and tracing the Chinese–English pairing of terms.
- [Book: Investments (Bodie, Kane & Marcus)](https://book.douban.com/subject/105.sd/)
  The global standard investments textbook (a designated CFA reference). Use it for: the backbone of the Investments branch (04) — risk and return, portfolio theory, CAPM, bonds and derivatives.
- [Book: Corporate Finance (Ross, Westerfield & Jaffe)](https://book.douban.com/subject_search?search_text=%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E7%90%86%E8%B4%A2%20Ross)
  The standard corporate finance textbook. Use it for: NPV, capital structure, dividend policy, valuation (branch 03).
- [Book: The Economics of Money, Banking and Financial Markets (Mishkin)](https://book.douban.com/subject_search?search_text=%E8%B4%A7%E5%B8%81%E9%87%91%E8%9E%8D%E5%AD%A6%20%E7%B1%B3%E4%BB%80%E9%87%91)
  The standard money and banking textbook. Use it for: money, interest rates, central banks, and the financial system (branches 01 and 02).
- [People's Bank of China (PBOC) · Monetary Policy Column](http://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhengcehuobisi/125207/125227/index.html)
  Primary-source policy material. Use it for: real data and announcements on China's monetary policy tools (LPR, MLF, the required reserve ratio).
- [Federal Reserve Education / FEDS Notes](https://www.federalreserve.gov/education.htm)
  The Federal Reserve's official educational material. Use it for: first-hand explanations of how a central bank operates and how the dollar system works.
- [IMF · Online Learning / Finance & Development](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/fandd)
  The IMF's bilingual Chinese–English finance magazine. Use it for: an authoritative introduction to international finance (branch 07) and macro-financial topics.
- [BIS (Bank for International Settlements) — annual reports and glossary entries](https://www.bis.org/)
  The "central banks' bank." Use it for: advanced topics such as the Basel Accords and macroprudential regulation (branches 06 and 02).
- [Khan Academy · Finance & Capital Markets](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/core-finance)
  Free video course, paced for absolute beginners. Use it for: video supplements on compounding, bond pricing, options, and similar topics.
- [Yale · Financial Markets (Robert Shiller, Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/financial-markets-global)
  The open course by Nobel laureate Robert Shiller. Use it for: a systematic capstone review after finishing the whole course.
- [CFP Board / the Asset Management Association of China (AMAC) investor-education column](https://www.amac.org.cn/)
  Use it for: the official investor-education line on personal finance (branch 10) and fund knowledge.

## Wisdom (communities)

- [Xueqiu](https://xueqiu.com/)
  A Chinese investor community with an average signal-to-noise ratio. Use it for: watching how real investors discuss risk and return, and mapping course concepts onto real conversations.
- [r/personalfinance](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/) and [r/Bogleheads](https://www.reddit.com/r/Bogleheads/)
  English-language personal-finance communities with exceptionally high-quality wikis. Use it for: real cases and consensus practices for the Personal Finance branch (10).
- [Chinese Financial Markets · Zhihu finance topic digest](https://www.zhihu.com/topic/19750531/hot)
  Use it for: regulation and market discussions in a Chinese context (watch out for marketing content).
