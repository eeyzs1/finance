Branch 04 · Investments

Investments

Investments comes down to one core question — how much risk are you willing to trade for how much return? This branch teaches you to state both “risk” and “return” as numbers: first learn to measure them (Lesson 1), then learn to press risk down with a portfolio (Lesson 2), and finally price risk with a straight line (Lesson 3).

After this branch, you can…

Read what “8% annualized, 15% volatility” on a fund page actually means; explain why you shouldn’t stake all your money on a single stock; use β to estimate how sensitive a stock is to the market’s ups and downs.
