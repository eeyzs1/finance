Insurance & Actuarial Science

Insurance swaps a rare large loss no individual could bear for a certain, budgetable small outlay; what makes it possible is not luck but a mathematical law — the law of large numbers. This branch explains that law, its pricing applications, and the boundary where it breaks down, all in one pass.

After this branch, you can…

Explain why one person’s misfortune cannot be predicted while 1,000 people’s misfortune can be priced; read the pure premium, deductible, and coinsurance inside a policy; recognize a “death spiral” and understand why health insurance mandates universal enrollment.
