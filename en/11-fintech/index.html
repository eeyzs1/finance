Branch 11 · Financial Technology

FinTech

Payments, lending, investing, insurance — every function of finance is being rewritten in software. This branch does not chase buzzwords; it walks you through the three most important transformations: the restructuring of the payments and clearing chain, blockchain and digital currencies, and robo-advisors with big-data risk control.

After this branch, you can…

① Explain the clearing chain behind a single QR-code payment, and why “payment successful” and “money received” are two different things; ② Explain how blockchain achieves “tamper-resistance” and where that ends, and tell Bitcoin apart from a central bank digital currency; ③ See what robo-advisors and big-data risk control actually do, and where the convenience stops and the risks begin.

The Three Core Lessons

Lesson 1The Evolution of Payments and Clearing

What happens the instant you scan a QR code: why information flow and funds flow separate, what each party in the four-party model earns, and how the e-CNY manages to move “peer-to-peer”.

About 15 minutes · Beginner
Lesson 2Blockchain and Digital Currencies

Why hashes and chained blocks are “tamper-resistant”, what consensus mechanisms cost, and the essential difference between Bitcoin and a central bank digital currency.

Best taken after Lesson 1
Lesson 3Robo-Advisors and Big-Data Risk Control

What an algorithm relies on when it allocates your assets, how big data draws your credit profile, and the model risk hiding behind the convenience.

Best taken after Lesson 2
