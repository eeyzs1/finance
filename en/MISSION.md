# Mission: General Finance & Financial Literacy

## Why

The learner wants sound financial judgment in real life: to understand the mechanics behind financial news (what a central-bank rate cut actually means), to make better personal financial decisions (how to allocate savings, which mortgage to choose, what insurance to buy), and to build an overall understanding of how the financial system works — not to pass exams or professional certifications.

## Success looks like

- Explain to a friend the full transmission chain of how one deposit becomes multiple times more money through the banking system;
- Look at a wealth-management product or fund, point out which risks its returns come from, and judge whether the fees are reasonable;
- Use the tools of compound interest, net present value (NPV), and expected value to make everyday money decisions (prepay the loan or invest; buy a given insurance policy or not);
- Read the key passages of a central-bank monetary policy report or a financial headline and know what every term refers to;
- Recognize common financial scams, as well as one's own psychological biases in financial decisions.

## Constraints

- Adult self-study in fragments of free time; 10–20 minutes per lesson;
- Everything in Simplified Chinese, with mathematics at high-school level only: intuition and diagrams first, light on derivations;
- The course material is local HTML pages that open offline; interactive self-study is the main mode;
- No finance background to start from — zero prior knowledge is assumed.

## Out of scope

- Exam-prep content for professional certifications such as CFA / FRM / CPA;
- Hands-on high-frequency quantitative trading and Python trading-system development;
- Practitioner-grade valuation modeling (DCF models linked to the three financial statements);
- Specific investment advice on individual stocks or cryptocurrencies (this course teaches frameworks only — no stock picks).
