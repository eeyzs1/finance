Finance Self-Study Course

11 main branches 33+ interactive lessons Works fully offline Best followed in the recommended learning path

Finance is the study of how funds are allocated across the two dimensions of time and risk. This course breaks the subject into 11 main branches, each with one knowledge map plus three interactive lessons: drag sliders to build intuition, take the quiz to lock in what you learned, and finish every lesson with one takeaway you can keep.

Recommended learning path

Foundations: 01 Financial Markets & Institutions → 02 Money & Banking; The Three Core Pillars: 04 Investments → 03 Corporate Finance → 08 Behavioral Finance; Advanced Toolkit: 05 Derivatives → 06 Risk Management → 07 International Finance; Putting It to Work: 10 Personal Finance → 09 Insurance & Actuarial Science → 11 FinTech.
