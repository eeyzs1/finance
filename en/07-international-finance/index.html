International Finance

The moment money crosses a border, it enters two markets at once — goods and currencies: to buy US stocks you must first buy US dollars, and to sell German machine tools you must first quote a price in euros. The exchange rate is the converter that links these two worlds. In three lessons, this branch explains how to read an exchange rate, what determines it, and how it reaches into a whole economy through the balance of payments.

After this branch, you can…

Understand how news of a renminbi appreciation or depreciation affects exporters, students studying abroad, and travelers in different ways; explain purchasing power parity with the Big Mac Index; and grasp the identity that a current account deficit must be matched by capital inflows.
